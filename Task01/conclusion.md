# Task 1 – Conclusion

The total estimated network traffic generated by one production line is **158.24 Mbps**, while eight production lines generate **1265.92 Mbps (1.26592 Gbps)**. CCTV cameras contribute the highest bandwidth consumption at 96 Mbps per line, whereas robot controllers have the strictest delay requirement of 10 ms.

A single 1 Gbps uplink is sufficient for one production line but insufficient for all eight lines under the given traffic assumptions. Sharing a FIFO queue between CCTV and robot control traffic can introduce queuing delay and jitter, potentially causing robot commands to miss their deadlines. Therefore, QoS-based traffic prioritisation is necessary.

With motion-triggered CCTV recording at 5% activity, the estimated traffic decreases to 67.04 Mbps per line and 536.32 Mbps for eight lines. This brings the estimated traffic within the nominal capacity of a 1 Gbps uplink. However, protocol overhead, traffic bursts, redundancy and future expansion must still be considered.
